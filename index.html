Leadership Profile

Allama Muhammad Uzair Al-Madni

Founder & Chief Trustee, Faizan-E-AhleBait International Trust for Humanitarian (FAITH)

format_quote

"True charity is not merely about handing out rations; it is about protecting the honor and dignity of our brothers and sisters in distress. Every rupee entrusted to Faizan-E-AhleBait International Trust for Humanitarian (FAITH) is an inviolable Amanah before Allah (SWT). We deliver at doorsteps under the cover of modesty, ensuring no heart feels diminished."

— Allama Muhammad Uzair Al-Madni Quetta

Allama Muhammad Uzair Al-Madni established Faizan-E-AhleBait International Trust for Humanitarian (FAITH) with a singular, unwavering resolve: to institutionalize transparent, zero-overhead charitable distribution inspired by the pristine teachings of the Ahle Bait.

Having witnessed firsthand the vulnerability of widows, neglected orphans, and daily wage earners during regional distress, he pioneered door-to-door ground verification. Under his leadership, the Trust has eliminated traditional middleman bureaucracies, ensuring that every grain of ration and every medical subsidy reaches verified beneficiaries directly.

Our Journey & Growth

From humble grassroots food distribution to a nationwide welfare network touching over 25,000 families annually through Faizan-E-AhleBait International Trust for Humanitarian (FAITH).

First Emergency Ration Drive

Allama Uzair organized the first grassroots ration distribution drive in Karachi under FAITH, providing essential monthly food packs to 50 destitute widow families out of personal funds.
